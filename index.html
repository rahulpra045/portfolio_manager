How this works

A walk-through of the screens: adding clients, running and importing reviews, choosing funds, and keeping the data safe.

The screens

  • Portfolio Reviews – one row per client showing their latest review. Filter by search, priority, action, "Reviewed as on" date and review month. Click a row to open that client's review history. The buttons at the top add a new review or import reviews from Excel.
  • Prepare Reviews – upload your client list and allocation report as you receive them; the app combines them into a sheet you check before any review is created (see below).
  • Client Master – the roster of clients (name, Client ID, PAN, DOB, address) with totals at the top (clients, reviewed, not yet reviewed). Reviews are always linked to a client here.
  • Policy Rules – the risk, horizon, age and transition tables that drive the target allocation. They are locked until you unlock them with the Security PIN.
  • Fund Master – the universe of funds that can be suggested.
  • Firm Settings – letterhead details for PDFs, the PAN rules switch, Automatic review inputs, the Security PIN, whether the Logs menu is shown, Application updates (desktop app) and Backup & Restore.
  • Logs – a permanent record of what was added, changed, deleted or imported, with date and time (see below).

On Portfolio Reviews, Client Master and Fund Master, click any column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse the order. An arrow shows the current sort, and a Reset sort button appears while a sort is active. The ✕ at the top of the left menu hides it; the ☰ button brings it back.

Adding a client

  1. Open Client Master and click + Add Client.
  2. Enter First name, Last name, Date of birth (required), and optionally Client ID, PAN and Address. A Client ID is generated if you leave it blank.
  3. Click Save Client. A Client ID or PAN that already belongs to another client is rejected.

To add many at once, use Import from Excel on the same screen and map your columns.

PAN rules (format AAAPL1234A and 5th letter = surname initial) can be switched off in Firm Settings. A blank PAN, or "0", is treated as no PAN. Children under 18 may have no PAN, or share a Client ID / PAN with a parent or sibling — each child is kept as a separate client, recognised by name and date of birth. For adults, a Client ID or PAN that already belongs to someone else is always rejected.

Running a review

New client + review together

  1. On Portfolio Reviews click + New Client & Review.
  2. Fill the client details, AUM (₹ Lakh), the AUM as-of date and the current allocation (Equity, Debt, Hybrid, Liquid – must total 100%).
  3. Click Save & Run Review.

New review for an existing client

  1. Open the client's history – search for them at the top of Portfolio Reviews, or use Client Master → History.
  2. Click + New Review, enter AUM, as-of date and current allocation (pre-filled from the last review's targets), and click Save Review.

Review inputs you choose (only if automatic inputs are off)

This applies only when Automatic review inputs is switched off in Firm Settings. When it is on (the default) the app works all of these out itself from the Policy Rules and you do not see this section. When it is off, the New Client & Review and New Review windows have a Manual review inputs section and all six are required:

  • Current Risk Profile and New Risk Profile – Conservative, Moderate, Aggressive, Very Aggressive. The current profile sets the target allocation; the new profile decides which funds are eligible.
  • Primary Goal – Retirement, Education, Wealth Creation, Other.
  • Goal Status – Good, Deficit.
  • Liquidity Reserve – Adequate, To be strengthened, Not assessed.
  • Reason Flagged – the eight "changes since last review" options printed on the PDF letter.

What the app calculates

  • Target allocation – the tightest of the risk, horizon and age limits from Policy Rules, moved gradually by the transition rules.
  • Review flag – a bucket (Equity / Debt / Hybrid) more than 10% away from target is "Action". 0 flags = Hold, 1 = Hold + Watchlist, 2 or more = Rebalance.
  • Investment period and the risk re-assessment block on page 3 of the PDF (ability / willingness to take risk, time horizon, liquidity need, outcome).

Choosing the funds

  1. Fill the form and click Save Review (or Save & Run Review).
  2. If any bucket needs more funds, a Select funds window opens before anything is saved. Each such bucket lists the eligible funds (eligible in Fund Master, suitable for the client's age and investment period, at or below the new risk profile). Use the search box and tick 1 to 3 funds per bucket.
  3. A bucket that needs no funds shows a fixed line instead: "Reduce existing fund exposure" when it must come down, "No change needed for this bucket", or "In line with portfolio" when fewer than two buckets need action.
  4. Click Save selection. Cancel brings you back to the form with nothing saved.

To change the picks later, open the client's history, expand the review and click Select Funds.

Dates on reviews

  • Last Reviewed – the day the review was actually saved.
  • AUM as-of – the date the AUM and allocation were calculated.
  • Review Date (PDF letter, CSV) – the client's birthday in the April–March year of the review. Next Review – the following birthday.

PDFs, exports and deleting

  • Open a client's history and click Generate PDF for one letter. Export All as PDFs (ZIP) creates one PDF per client in the current list. Files are named CLIENT_NAME_PAN_2026-27.pdf (Client ID is used when there is no PAN; the year is the April–March session of the review).
  • Export CSV downloads the current reviews with all fields.
  • Tick the checkboxes on Portfolio Reviews, Client Master or Fund Master and click Delete selected. On Portfolio Reviews only the shown review of each row is deleted; on Client Master the client and all their reviews are deleted.
  • Backup & Restore in Firm Settings exports or imports everything as one JSON file.

Importing reviews

  • Import Reviews from Excel – one row per review in the app's format. Rows are matched to a client by Client ID or PAN; a client that does not exist is created from the Name and DOB columns. Percentages can be written as 95.4 or 0.954 — the app checks that the current allocation adds up to 100%.
  • Import Old Data – loads past reviews from your calculated Excel sheet exactly as they are in the file; nothing is recalculated. A client that already has a review for the same AUM month is skipped, so importing the same file twice adds nothing. You can add an optional note that is saved in the Logs.
  • After either import you see how many reviews were added (and how many new clients were created) and a list of skipped rows with the reason for each.

Prepare Reviews (two Excel files)

  1. Open Prepare Reviews and choose the client list file (name, PAN, Client ID, DOB, address…) and the allocation report file.
  2. Choose the birth month: only clients whose date of birth falls in that month are prepared (pick All months to prepare everyone). Allocation rows of clients born in other months are left out.
  3. The AUM as on date is read from the report's file name (for example Allocation_Report_31_10_2026). Change it if it is wrong.
  4. The app converts the allocation to Equity / Debt / Hybrid: Equity + Sol Oriented + 50% of Others becomes Equity; Debt stays Debt; Hybrid + 50% of Others becomes Hybrid. AUM is converted from rupees to lakh.
  5. Check the combined sheet. It has one row per client, showing the client's DOB, PAN and Client ID. Where the same name appears more than once, the row is for the client born in the chosen month and its drop-down lists every allocation row with that name (AUM and Equity / Debt / Hybrid) — choose the right one, or skip the client. Suggest by closest last AUM can help. The import stays disabled until every such row is resolved.
  6. Click any column heading to sort (Reset sort clears it). The heading row stays in view while you scroll.
  7. Use Reject on any row you do not want (Restore brings it back), Download combined sheet to keep a copy, or Reject all to start again. Nothing is saved until you click Approve & create reviews.

Logs

  • Every addition, change, deletion and import is recorded with the date and time and a short line saying what happened. Use the filters, the Columns menu (the long Details column is hidden by default) and Export.
  • Deleting log entries needs the Security PIN, and the deletion itself leaves no trace. A wrong PIN attempt is recorded.
  • The Logs menu item can be hidden in Firm Settings; activity is still recorded in the background.

Security PIN and locks

  • Set one Security PIN (4–8 digits) in Firm Settings. Changing it later asks for the current one.
  • It is needed to delete log entries and to unlock Policy Rules. Policy Rules lock again after you save, after 5 minutes, when you leave the screen, or when you click Lock now.
  • If no PIN has been set, those actions ask you to set one first.

The desktop app

  • Exit application (bottom of the left menu) stops the app. Closing the window with the ✕ does the same after a few seconds. On the main laptop it waits while other laptops are still working.
  • Updates – an Update available message appears when a newer version is published; click Update now. In Firm Settings → Application updates you can also check manually, install an update from a file, or Roll back to the previous version. Updates never touch your data.
  • Other laptops connect to the main laptop and follow it, so they always run the same version.

Shared data

Clients, reviews, Policy Rules, Fund Master and Firm Settings are shared with everyone who uses this app, and edits sync live.
